The Importance of Good Web Design

By Augesta G | August 2026

A well-designed webpage helps users find information quickly and comfortably. Good typography, proper spacing, and a clean layout improve readability and create a better user experience.

Choosing the Right Font

Serif fonts such as Georgia are often used for articles because they are easy to read in long paragraphs. Using a larger font size and sufficient spacing between lines prevents eye strain and makes the content look professional.

Using Proper Line Spacing

The line-height property controls the vertical spacing between lines of text. A value around 1.6 to 1.8 is commonly used for article content because it provides comfortable reading on both desktop and mobile devices.

By combining readable fonts, balanced spacing, and a simple layout, you can create article pages that are attractive, accessible, and easy for visitors to enjoy.
